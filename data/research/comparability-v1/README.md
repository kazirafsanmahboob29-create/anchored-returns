October 31, 2026

The [study design](../../../docs/comparability_plan_2026-10-31.md) fixes Phase 1. The [research note](../../../reports/comparability/README.md) presents the question and roster in readable form.

| File | Contents |
|---|---|
| `pilot.json` | The 15 selected proceedings, dates, entities to match, reasons and prior exposure |
| `screening.json` | All 42 candidates considered in this bounded phase and their classifications |
| `sources.json` | Original sources, locating records, retrieval status and evidence level |
| `design-freeze.json` | Fingerprints of the dated plan, dictionary and records at Phase 1 completion |

Source PDFs are cached separately from the distributable source package. A relative local path identifies the source's expected place in a restored local research archive. It does not mean the PDF is included here. Locating records identify a proceeding; numerical verification requires the relevant original decision or filing.

Phase 1 contains design and identity metadata. The [Phase 2 records](phase2/README.md) now add original dispositions for all 15 proceedings, approved financial inputs, request positions and source locations. Extraction follows the [field dictionary](../../../docs/comparability_fields.md). Amendments preserve this initial roster and identify any changed cases, rules or source versions.

The frozen files remain unchanged. Phase 2 stores fact records, request positions, approved schedules and new source-acquisition records separately. Progress reports join those records to the fixed proceeding identifiers without rewriting the original selection or its source-access status.
