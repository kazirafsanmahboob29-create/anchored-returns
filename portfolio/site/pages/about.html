I’m Kazi Rafsan Mahboob, a student at Dartmouth College. I grew up in Dhaka, Bangladesh and am interested in mathematics, economics and how people make decisions.

My current project, Anchored Returns, studies how utility commissions set authorized returns on equity. I use rate-case records and financial data to examine the role of market conditions and earlier awards.

This site brings together my projects, coursework, papers and photography.
