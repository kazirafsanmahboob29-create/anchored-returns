About me

I’m a student at Dartmouth College, originally from Dhaka, Bangladesh. My interests include mathematics, economics and how people make decisions.

My current research looks at how utility commissions set returns on equity. In Anchored Returns, I compare earlier awards with interest rates and other financial conditions using rate-case records and economic data.
