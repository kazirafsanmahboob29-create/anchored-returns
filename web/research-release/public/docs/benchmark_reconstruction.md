The frozen v0.1.3 sampling and coding protocol remains unchanged. The benchmark still uses the same service, excludes limited-issue riders and describes the most recent complete half-year before the written order.</p><p>I preserve every individual decision row, its service, written date, reported ROE, rider flag and native-text evidence. Rows without an ROE remain in the source table and do not enter the arithmetic mean. I exclude only the publisher&#x27;s LIR-designated rider rows from this national comparison. The pilot&#x27;s contested-case criteria do not change the national benchmark population.</p><p>Before calculating a half-year, I reconcile the complete extraction with the publisher&#x27;s annual numeric counts and means, both with and without riders. I retain the included row IDs, sum and unrounded mean. Decimal arithmetic rounds half up to two percentage decimals to match the printed series; this introduces at most 0.5 basis point of rounding difference. I never subtract rounded annual and first-half means to infer a second-half mean.</p><p>The registry labels this method <code>case_level_reconstruction</code>. Its <code>printed_percent</code> remains null because the half-year mean is calculated rather than printed. The <code>display_percent</code> holds the rounded comparison value. Publisher-reported means retain their own method and original publication vintage. A report published after an order cannot be evidence available to that commission at the written date.</p><p>The implemented calculation uses the original January 31, 2020 edition&#x27;s 2019 Table 5, with data compiled January 29. It checks the report identity, date, LIR definition, complete native extraction and four annual count/mean controls. The <a href="../reports/sample_and_benchmarks_2026-10-04.md" rel="noopener noreferrer">review</a> reports the resulting H2 2019 means, source pages and remaining gaps. The <a href="../analysis/README.md" rel="noopener noreferrer">reproduction guide</a> explains the complete CSV/DTA decision-table export.</p></main></body></html>
