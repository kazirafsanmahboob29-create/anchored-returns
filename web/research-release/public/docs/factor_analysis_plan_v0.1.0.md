The earlier period comparisons were already known. This is an analysis plan, not a preregistration.</p><h2>Questions</h2><ol><li>How are authorized electric utility ROEs associated with interest rates, inflation, broad financial stress, filing duration and case type?</li><li>Does the previous observed award for the same utility-state label add information after accounting for those variables?</li><li>Do the associations persist with state and year controls, utility-state and year controls, and within-group changes?</li><li>Do models fitted through 2019 improve on simple benchmarks when predicting recorded 2020–2024 awards?</li></ol><h2>Population and timing</h2><p>Use the preserved EEI 2025 Q3 electric table, completed distribution and vertically integrated cases only. The estimation period is 2010–2024, excluding the incomplete 2025 reporting year. A table observation is not necessarily a distinct contested proceeding. Preserve source worksheet rows, missing awards, utility labels and docket identifiers.</p><p>Use the October 26, 2026 historical macroeconomic vintage. These are reconstructed historical measurements, not an as-known information set. In particular, STLFSI4 is a revised index. Observation lags reduce accidental use of later dates but do not remove historical revisions.</p><ul><li>Treasury: arithmetic mean DGS10 over the 90 calendar days strictly before authorization; require at least 40 numeric observations and a last observation no more than seven days before authorization. Repeat with 30 days, at least ten observations.</li><li>Yield curve: T10Y3M over the same windows and coverage rules, in percentage points.</li><li>Inflation: CPIAUCNS year-over-year percentage change for the month two calendar months before authorization. Require both month levels. This is a conservative calendar lag, not a reconstruction of release timestamps.</li><li>Financial stress: latest STLFSI4 observation dated at least 14 days before authorization, no more than 14 days before that cutoff. Units are index points. This is broad financial stress, not a utility credit spread or equity risk premium.</li><li>Filing duration: elapsed days from request to authorization, divided by 365.25. Invalid, negative or greater-than-ten-year durations remain missing and are counted.</li><li>Prior award: mean reported award at the most recent strictly earlier authorization date for the same exact utility label and state, no more than ten years earlier. Same-day entries never supply one another&#x27;s prior award. Preserve prior row IDs, date, multiplicity and gap. This proxy measures recorded persistence, not a commission&#x27;s use of precedent.</li><li>Case type: distribution indicator. Do not infer settlement status from the publisher&#x27;s <code>Settled</code> label.</li></ul><h2>Estimation</h2><p>The dependent variable is authorized ROE in percentage points. Main models share the complete-case sample for the four macro variables, filing duration and prior award. Estimate (1) market and case variables, (2) add prior award, (3) state and authorization-year fixed effects, and (4) exact utility-state and year fixed effects. Drop redundant constant categorical terms; fail on remaining rank deficiency. Report sample counts and exclusions, coefficients, R-squared and 95% normal-approximation Wald intervals with finite-sample-corrected two-way cluster covariance by state and authorization quarter. State clustering covers dependence within commissions and repeated utilities; quarter clustering allows common shocks. Display nonpositive variance estimates as unavailable, never replace them with zero. Report HC3 uncertainty as a sensitivity check.</p><p>Report Pearson and Spearman correlations with pairwise sample counts, and the main common-sample correlation matrix separately. Correlations and fitted slopes are associations. A causal claim would additionally require an identification design and credible exogeneity assumptions.</p><h2>Checks fixed in advance</h2><ul><li>Replace 90-day market windows with 30-day windows, holding the main sample fixed where possible.</li><li>Omit all New England observations; restrict to distribution cases.</li><li>Add requested ROE in a separate complete-pair analysis, comparing models on the same restricted sample. The request is jointly determined with the proceeding; its coefficient is not a structural effect.</li><li>Difference successive observations within exact utility-state groups in the estimation window, with gaps no greater than ten years; report both pooled and year-adjusted change regressions.</li><li>Fit pooled market-and-case and prior-award-augmented models using 2010–2019 only. Evaluate on complete 2020–2024 rows against the training mean and last-observed-award baselines. Report MAE and RMSE in basis points, including negative improvements. This is a single temporal holdout, with no hyperparameter search.</li><li>Recalculate core coefficients and correlations in native R from the exported panel and compare numerically with Python. Supply native Stata syntax without claiming Stata execution.</li></ul><h2>Data refresh and publication</h2><p>Keep the archived paper inputs fixed and hash every input. A separate tool can refresh the four whitelisted FRED series, preserve an entire complete retrieval, rebuild the dated joins and rerun selected calculations. A failed or incomplete refresh must not replace a previous complete snapshot. A refresh updates the macro vintage, not the EEI case population. No synthetic observations, imputed awards or invented independent ratings enter this analysis.</p></main></body></html>
