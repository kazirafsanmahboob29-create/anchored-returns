Include original final electric/gas decisions dated January 1, 2019 through the freeze date; exclude pending matters, meeting votes without written reasons, mechanical updates and rehearings as separate observations.</li><li>Retain 15 orders, all six states represented, maximum four per state, at least five 2019–2021, at least five 2023 onward, at least two Vermont setting/formula orders, and NH DE 24-070 included by design.</li><li>Search official commission rate-case indexes and docket records. Log every considered decision, its procedural classification and the evidence for inclusion/exclusion. A failed download is an unresolved record, not an eligibility exclusion.</li><li>Use litigated decisions; partial settlements are eligible when the ROE issue is decided on the record rather than stipulated. A shared recommendation does not itself establish a formal settlement. Apply the existing settled-substitute rule only after the state search supports it, and identify substitutes explicitly. If these rules cannot produce 15, report infeasibility rather than quietly relaxing them.</li><li>Among eligible decisions, prefer the latest written dates subject to all quotas. Operationally, sort descending by date, then ascending state, docket and utility; choose the lexicographically earliest feasible 15-element subsequence. Quota-driven older selections and any unresolved more-recent candidates must be visible in the log. Do not rank by award, request gap or observed reasoning.</li><li>A distinct original final written order is an observation. Record those actual dates separately and retain dated releases; the cutoff governs decision eligibility, not the date work was performed.</p><h2>Coding clarifications</h2><ul><li><code>OWN</code> takes precedence for this commission&#x27;s own prior authorized returns, including its awards for another in-state utility. <code>PEER</code> means authorized returns of other commissions. A citation to an earlier case solely for a legal/model rule is not an ROE-history comparison.</li><li>Preserve the 11 original codes and the paragraph as printed as the unit. Split mixed party/commission voice, preserve cross-page continuations and footnotes, and retain page/offset provenance.
