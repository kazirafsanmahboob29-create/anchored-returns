Run `./research refresh-treasury` when current Treasury data are needed. It downloads a new immutable bundle under `data/external/treasury/`, preserves the raw response and missing values, converts percent to fraction with decimal arithmetic, and writes the selected original-order matches. Earlier snapshots and the original analysis CSV stay unchanged. Refreshes are explicit; there is no background scheduler. The running local site provides a Refresh Treasury sources control that downloads both official feeds, compares them, regenerates the analysis and retains previous snapshots. Start it with `./research serve`. A failed refresh restores the previous complete active release.

Date matching never uses a future observation. Weekend/holiday/missing observations can use the latest earlier nonmissing value within the requested coverage. Each match records its actual date and calendar-day gap. A gap exceeding seven days retains the observed evidence but withholds the analytical value. Dates before the snapshot’s first observation or after its requested end are unavailable. A retrieval snapshot does not establish which revisions were available at a historical decision date.

`./research verify-treasury` downloads the official annual XML responses for years needed by the entered original-order dates. It checks feed identity, date ranges, duplicates, null values and decimal conversion, then records each overlapping-date difference without replacing either series. The current six years are 2020, 2021, 2023, 2024, 2025 and 2026. The comparison does not claim coverage of every historical year or statistical independence of the underlying yield measurement. Refresh this comparison after changing the active FRED snapshot. Raw XML and normalization remain under `data/external/treasury_official/`.

Both primary webpages and the downloaded feeds were checked on October 3. FRED's latest observation is October 1 at 5.24%, while Treasury's is October 2 at 5.28%. This is a difference in the feeds' available dates, not a same-date disagreement. The project preserves and labels each value separately.

I record reviewed official links, expected identity, dates and discovery notes in the acquisition queue. Supporting documents retain their specific roles and event dates; draft minutes and applications are not automatically classified as final orders. The downloader accepts the listed commission hosts and individually reviewed redirects, requires an intact PDF, reuses M1/M2 and never replaces cataloged bytes. It checks saved files on repeat calls. PDF acquisition is not a procedural eligibility decision. New links still require reading the source page and decision.

The national benchmark definition is the same-service mean authorized return, excluding riders, for the most recent completed half-year before an order. The public H1 2026 means are 9.79% for electric and 9.64% for gas. Their publisher describes a 2026 expansion of coverage but does not identify the dataset version for every headline mean. They are retained in a separate reference catalog pending version confirmation. The September 3 publication also postdates the August 28 Vermont order; it would be a retrospective comparison, not evidence available to that commission.

The [historical registry](../data/external/rra_historical_benchmarks.json) preserves H1 2020, 2021, 2022 and 2024 electric and gas values from original report vintages. The [coverage report](../reports/benchmarks_review_2026-10-04.json) identifies each exact match and missing required period. Two 2025 Kentucky links now return removal/confidentiality notices, so their cached search text is not entered as verified data. The original CMP public/redacted order was retrieved through Maine’s ordinary public document-opening form and registered through M1/M2.

Searches also located annual, trailing-year and rider-undefined means for earlier periods. Those do not fill the required historical benchmark fields. I have no licensed S&P/RRA access, confirmed October 3. A library listing for Capital IQ does not establish RRA entitlement. No suitable S&P/RRA connector was found in the available plugin search. Public commission copies remain an acquisition route; no paid API is connected. The [access-request drafts](research_access_requests.md) specify the missing materials, without assuming a subscription or sending correspondence.

I can continue the 15-order measurement pilot. A meaningful pilot still needs its quota-constrained sample, complete coding, documented numeric missingness and reliability observations. Several hundred orders and a longer, comparable panel would be needed for the proposed full-study rate-response analysis; connecting a feed alone cannot supply that design.
