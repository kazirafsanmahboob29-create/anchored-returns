# Expanded document cohort, version 0.1.4

Kazi Rafsan Mahboob | October 6, 2026

I expand the study from a proposed 15-order pilot to a fixed cohort of 23 written decisions. The primary cohort contains all 22 eligible decisions in the screened public-document library. One Rhode Island settlement decision forms a separate supplemental stratum. This amendment changes the sampling design; it does not certify completion of the six-state decision census.

The cohort covers written decisions dated January 1, 2019 through October 1, 2026. Admission depends on a preserved original document and an eligibility determination supported by exact page text. I admit all screened eligible decisions within that window, without choosing cases by their ROE, benchmark availability or coding results. The manifest retains the screening file fingerprint and source fingerprints. Future additions require a new manifest version.

The primary stratum includes contested ROE determinations, uncontested determinations supported by the record, and orders establishing an ROE formula. Formula-setting orders without a numerical award remain available for textual analysis but contribute no award observation. Routine formula implementations and orders without an ROE determination remain excluded. The Rhode Island settlement is available for separate comparison; the default analysis excludes it.

Distinct utility-specific orders are distinct document observations. The two Connecticut gas orders share a proceeding and substantial reasoning. The joint Fitchburg order is one observation with two services. Related Vermont plan and rate orders are assigned shared proceeding groups. Counts of documents, proceedings, numerical awards and completed coded sections are reported separately.

The original v0.1.3 protocol and 15-order proposal remain archived as a pilot specification. Its quotas and census prerequisites do not define this expanded cohort. The new design is a bounded public-document cohort, not a random sample or an exhaustive census. Missing orders and uneven state coverage limit generalization. Cohort membership is finalized for this release; numeric entry, complete-section coding and independent reliability are separate completion criteria.

National comparisons use the preceding complete half-year, the same utility service, general rate cases excluding limited-issue riders, and an identified report vintage. A benchmark published after an order is retrospective context and is excluded from contemporaneous differences. Annual, rolling-year, quarterly and enhanced-coverage means cannot fill a legacy half-year gap. Public summary observations are retained separately if they cannot meet these conditions.

Exploratory selections in the analysis workspace do not amend the cohort. Exports identify the selected records, denominator, missingness, source links, dataset date and calculation method. No causal interpretation or independent coding agreement is inferred from descriptive comparisons.

## October 20 data extension

A separate EEI electric series supplies retrospective, case-weighted half-year comparisons from a public workbook. Base-rate observations exclude riders and transmission cases and reconcile against the workbook's quarterly controls. This alternative does not replace the strict RRA series or establish that a comparator was available to a commission at its written decision date. The analysis workspace reports the selected series explicitly.
