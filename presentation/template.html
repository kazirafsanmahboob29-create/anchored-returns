Calculation details and missingness

Percent differences are computed in basis points. Both national series use the same service and preceding complete half-year. RRA differences require publication by the decision date. The separate EEI series is reconstructed from individual electric rate cases and is always retrospective. Differences use the displayed two-decimal national mean. Joint-service orders can supply two service comparisons. Missing requests and formula-only orders are not assigned values.

What I am measuring
I distinguish market evidence, other commissions’ awards, the commission’s own prior awards, stability, legal standards and other explanations. A reference to precedent is recorded with its stance; a code flag alone does not show reliance.
The expanded cohort includes every eligible order in the screened document library: 22 primary decisions, one supplemental Rhode Island settlement and four formula-implementation orders in a separate supplement. The original 15-order quota proposal is preserved as a pilot comparison. This bounded cohort does not establish a complete six-state census.
What a reader can verify
Every passage retains its exact source text, page and offsets. Numeric entries retain printed units, attribution and conversions. Original awards stay separate from later modifications; missing data remain visible.

Interpretation
The document records support comparisons of recorded awards and close reading of the reasons given. State averages combine different services, years and regulatory mechanisms and cannot identify the effect of a state’s regulatory policy. Coding assistance and coder identity are documented in the ledger; independent reliability has not been measured. Source-integrity checks establish reproducibility, not independent agreement.
