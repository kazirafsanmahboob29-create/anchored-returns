## Central Maine Power

The [February 19, 2020 order](https://mpuc-cms.maine.gov/CQM.Public.WebUI/MatterManagement/MatterFilingItem.aspx?FilingSeq=105431&CaseNumber=2018-00194) matches the utility, docket, decision date and 8.25% award. Printed page 118, PDF page 123, states the commission's approval of a 100-basis-point management-efficiency adjustment for customer-service failures. Printed page 137, PDF page 142, repeats the operative award and adjustment in the ordering provisions. Both pages have been checked against the local PDF.

The model predicted 9.357366%, an overprediction of 110.736608 basis points. Adding the documented 100-basis-point adjustment back to the final award gives 9.25%, a difference of 10.736608 basis points from that prediction. This is a transparent arithmetic comparison, not a refitted model or a revised target.

The order explicitly links the reduction to management and customer service. It explains why the applied award is much lower than the selected financing allowance. The [financial pilot](../reports/comparability/README.md) examines this distinction alongside capital structure in other selected proceedings. Source identity and page locations remain in the evidence records.
